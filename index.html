How to play

  1. 01A category is called. Type words that belong in it.
  2. 02Each word you set pulls the brass slug toward your plate.
  3. 03Longer words pull harder. Use the Hot Letter to double the pull.
3–4 letters+1
5–7 letters+2
8+ letters+3
Contains the Hot Letter×2 (max 6)

Each word can be set once per set, by either plate. The Foreman can strike a word to reverse its pull. First to the edge wins the set; best of three takes the match.
