**Full version:**

```
Play alone right now: enter a name, tap Practice alone, and a bot printer takes the Black Plate.

Pressroom Pull is a real-time word tug-of-war for 2–12 players, set in a Victorian print shop. Two teams, the Red Plate and the Black Plate, fight over one strip of paper. The Foreman calls a category, and every valid word your team types drags the brass slug along the composing rule toward your edge. Three to four letters pull 1, five to seven pull 2, eight or more pull 3, and a random Hot Letter doubles the pull. First to the edge wins the set; best of three takes the match.

The twist is the type case. Like a real letterpress shop, each plate sets its words from a tray of metal letters. Every word uses its letters up, and the type is only put back ten seconds later. Spend all your E's on one long word and the press refuses your next one: you're out of sorts. There are only two of the Hot Letter per printer, so doubling is rationed. Fast teams have to think about which word to spend their letters on, and when to reach for the rare J, Q, X, Z, K and V.

Built for classrooms, office icebreakers and noisy friend nights: it rewards vocabulary, quick thinking and teamwork, and anyone can join in seconds.

Details:
• Join from any phone or laptop with a four-letter code. No login, no install.
• The type case sits under your input and lights up the letters your word needs, and the ones you're missing, before you press Enter.
• The Foreman can strike a bad word: its pull reverses and its type goes straight back into the case.
• Uneven teams are balanced automatically: the smaller plate pulls +1 per word.
• A "Your move" bar tells every player what to do right now.
• Each set ends with the Pressroom Gazette: longest word, printer of the set, best type, and how often each plate ran out of sorts.
• Printer's ranks from Printer's Devil to Proprietor, eight engraved medals and a shareable result card.
```

## Quick start for a judge (about 3 minutes)

1. Open the link, type a name, tap **Practice alone**, then **Start the press run**.
2. Read the category and the Hot Letter during the 5-second preview.
3. Type words that fit the category and press Enter. Watch the slug move toward the red edge.
4. Watch the type case under the input: letters you need light up, and missing ones turn red. If a word is refused as "out of sorts", try one with different letters.
5. Win two sets to take the match, then read the Gazette.
